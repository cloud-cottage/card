项目介绍

CARD（Chinese Ancestral Repository of Descendants，中华族裔人物资料库）是依托历代家谱史料构建的开放人物谱系数据库。

哈佛大学CBDB（中国历代人物传记资料库）以正史、碑刻、文集传记为核心；CARD与其形成互补，核心原始资料为家谱，收录古往今来华人、海内外华裔谱系人物记录。

本项目向公众开放：

  • 简易姓名检索：输入人名查询谱系人物档案
  • 机器可读结构化数据：人物记录支持JSON格式获取
  • 开放API：支持程序调用，面向数字人文、学术研究

声明：所有记录转录自家谱古籍，内容为史料原文记载，不等于客观史实。在世人物严格遵守隐私法规，不存储未经授权的个人隐私信息。

About the Project

CARD (Chinese Ancestral Repository of Descendants) is an open lineage database built on historical Chinese genealogies.

While the China Biographical Database (CBDB) focuses on official histories, epitaphs and literary biographies, CARD serves as its complement. Our primary source is family genealogies, preserving records of historical Chinese figures and overseas Chinese descendants.

Public access available:

  • Name search: Query lineage records by personal name
  • Machine-readable data: Each person entry available in JSON
  • Open API: Programmatic access for digital humanities & academic research

Disclaimer: Records are transcribed from genealogical manuscripts. Entries reflect source texts, not absolute historical truth. For living persons, we comply with privacy laws; no unauthorized private data will be stored.
